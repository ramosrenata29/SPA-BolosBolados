## Brand & Style

This design system expresses the warmth, craft, and refinement of modern high-end baking. The aesthetic merges tactile artisanal charm with crisp, contemporary digital execution. It avoids nostalgic clutter in favor of an airy, elevated editorial feel that honors ingredient quality and culinary precision.

### Personality & Tone
- **Sensory & Inviting:** Warm, buttery undertones, textured visual rhythm, and natural tones that evoke roasted cocoa, caramelized sugar, and botanical accents.
- **Crafted & Precise:** Clean lines, structured typographic pacing, and delicate containment that signal craftsmanship rather than industrial mass-production.
- **Uncluttered & Modern:** Generous whitespace, refined micro-borders, and quiet elevation that allow vibrant product photography and confection details to lead.

### Design Movement
**Modern Artisanal Minimalist:** Balanced proportions, bespoke typography pairings, warm atmospheric neutrals, and subtle, tactile depth cues.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the rich tones of slow baking—terracotta, espresso, and butter-washed dough—punctuated by fresh confectionery accents.

### Roles & Application
- **Primary (`#A13700`):** Anchors brand touchpoints, primary actions, prominent labels, and key focus indicators.
- **Secondary / Coral (`#FA5F57`):** Highlights seasonal specials, promotional tags, urgency cues, and active wishlist/favorite states.
- **Tertiary / Accent Emerald (`#1BB890`):** Denotes freshness, dietary/vegan highlights, order progress steps, and affirmative status indicators.
- **Supporting Gold (`#D48B00`):** Used for star ratings, awards, bakery certifications, and limited-edition product flags.
- **Supporting Forest (`#256C4B`):** Employed for sustainability messaging, organic badges, and deep natural contrast accents.
- **Surfaces & Grounds:** `#FDFBF7` serves as the canvas ground, mimicking raw unbleached parchment. Cards and overlays use `#FFFFFF` with `#EFE8DC` boundaries to maintain high product contrast.
- **Typography:** `#2D1A10` provides soft, warm espresso contrast without the harshness of pure black, supported by `#7A6960` for secondary descriptions and specifications.

## Typography

The pairing combines the architectural, geometric presence of Montserrat for titles and price points with the elegant, airy rhythm of Raleway for continuous descriptive reading.

### Rules of Usage
- **Display & Headings:** Always set in Montserrat. Track headings tight (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to maintain editorial punch and prevent large titles from separating.
- **Body & Captions:** Set in Raleway. Use Regular (`400`) and Medium (`500`) weights. Due to Raleway’s expressive geometric shapes, line height must never drop below `1.5` to maintain optical flow.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Montserrat in Semibold (`600`) with slight positive tracking (`+0.04em` to `+0.06em`) for buttons, dietary indicators, category flags, and tabs.
- **Numbers & Prices:** Montserrat Bold. Numerals must align neatly on baselines with distinct, legible currency symbols.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on an 8pt base grid with a 12-column responsive fluid grid capped at a maximum width of `1280px`. 

### Breakpoints & Geometry
- **Mobile (`< 768px`):** 4 columns, `1rem` margins, `1rem` gutters. Single-column card cascades, bottom-sheet overlays, and sticky quick-action purchase bars.
- **Tablet (`768px - 1024px`):** 8 columns, `1.5rem` margins, `1.5rem` gutters. 2-column confection catalogs, split product detail views.
- **Desktop (`> 1024px`):** 12 columns, centered container up to `1280px`, `1.5rem` gutters. 3 or 4-column product catalogs, persistent slide-out checkout drawers.

### Rhythmic Rules
- **Component Padding:** Standard cards use `1.5rem` internal clearance. Interactive inputs and buttons use `0.75rem` vertical and `1.25rem` horizontal padding.
- **Section Breaks:** Minimum `4.5rem` (`space-3xl`) vertical spacing between promotional bands and catalog shelves to preserve editorial breathability.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated with organic softness. Sharp drop-shadows are avoided in favor of warm ambient diffuse shadows paired with gossamer borders.

### Surface Architecture
- **Base Level (Canvas):** Pure `#FDFBF7` parchment tone, ground layer.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Tiles):** Solid `#FFFFFF` fill enclosed by a `1px` border of `#EFE8DC`, supported by an ambient drop shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(45, 26, 16, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover States & Active Dropdowns):** Subtle upward translation (`-2px`) with intensified warm shadow: `0 12px 30px -4px rgba(45, 26, 16, 0.08)`, border tint shifting to `#DECFC2`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Sticky Drawer & Cart):** Solid `#FFFFFF`, ambient shadow: `0 24px 48px -8px rgba(45, 26, 16, 0.16)`. Accompanied by a semi-transparent espresso backdrop: `rgba(45, 26, 16, 0.45)` with `4px` backdrop blur.

## Shapes

The interface balances organic culinary softness with clean digital architecture. A foundational roundedness level of `2` provides comfortable hand-feel without looking child-like or cartoonish.

### Radii Mapping
- **Input Fields & Buttons:** `0.5rem` (`rounded-base`).
- **Cards & Visual Containers:** `1rem` (`rounded-lg`).
- **Pills, Dietary Badges & Chips:** Fully circular pill shapes (`9999px`).
- **Modal Containers & Sheets:** `1.5rem` (`rounded-xl`) on all corners or top edges for bottom-sheets.

## Components

All iconography is strictly rendered using **Material Symbols Outlined** (`font-variation-settings: 'FILL' 0, 'wght' 400`).

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#A13700`, text `#FFFFFF`, font Montserrat Semibold. Subtly deepens to `#872E00` on hover. No hard outlines.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, `1.5px` border in `#A13700`, text `#A13700`. Hover brings a soft wash of `#FCEEE8`.
- **Tertiary / Fresh Action:** Background `#1BB890`, text `#FFFFFF`. Used for checkout actions, cart completion, or rapid order triggers.
- **Ghost:** Background transparent, text `#2D1A10`. Hover state reveals `#F7F3EC` fill.

### Chips & Badges
- **Dietary & Attribute Badges (Gluten-Free, Vegan, Nut-Free):** Pill-shaped, `0.75rem` text. Tinted background (`#E7F8F4` with `#256C4B` text, or `#FEECEB` with `#FA5F57` text).
- **Filter Chips:** `#FFFFFF` ground with `#EFE8DC` border. When selected: `#A13700` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.

### Cards (Pastry & Cake Showcases)
- Solid `#FFFFFF` background, `1rem` border-radius, `1px` `#EFE8DC` border.
- Image aspect ratio fixed at `4:3` or `1:1`, overflow hidden, subtle zoom on card hover (`scale(1.03)` over `300ms`).
- Content stack: Category label (`0.75rem` uppercase Montserrat), Item title (Montserrat Semibold), short description (Raleway Regular, muted), and lower row pairing price in bold with a primary icon button (`add_shopping_cart`).

### Form Controls & Inputs
- **Text Fields:** `0.5rem` radius, `1px` `#DECFC2` border, filled with `#FFFFFF`. Placeholder text `#7A6960`. Active/Focus state uses `#A13700` border with an outer ring of `rgba(161, 55, 0, 0.15)`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Rounded for checkboxes (`4px`), circular for radios. Inactive border `#DECFC2`. Checked fill `#A13700` with white glyph/inner dot.

### Specialized Bakery Components
- **Cake Customizer Stepper:** Horizontal progress line in `#EFE8DC` with active nodes rendered in `#A13700` and completed nodes in `#1BB890` containing `check`.
- **Flavor & Tier Swatches:** Circular radio selector disks (`44px`) displaying color/texture fills with a double ring on selection.
- **Mini-Cart Drawer:** Right-rail modal sliding over the viewport, displaying itemized list, quantity incrementers (`remove` and `add` symbols), and an espresso summary card anchored at the base.
